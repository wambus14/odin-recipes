(microwavable)Mac and Cheese

Description

Very cheesy, very yummy, very easy to make

Ingredients

  • Cup of microwavable macaroni and cheese
  • Tap water

Steps

  1. Open container, take out cheese packet
  2. Fill with water up to the line
  3. Stir
  4. Put in microwave, uncovered,for time specified on container
  5. Take out of microwave, mix cheese from packet in
  6. Enjoy!
